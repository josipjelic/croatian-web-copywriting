# Jezični savjetnik — česte dvojbe i zamke

A curated lookup of the specific spelling, word-choice, and punctuation rulings that
trip up Croatian web copy. Principles live in `duh-hrvatskog.md`; **this file is the
"which form is correct" reference** — consult it when you hesitate over a specific word
or mark.

Distilled from the *Jezični savjetnik* of Verbarium / lektoriranje.org (author Isidora
Vujošević) — an authoritative Croatian proofreading practice — and re-expressed with
copy-oriented examples. Only the ~30 entries most relevant to business/web copy are
included; the source has 250+ more (foreign-name declension, etymologies) worth a look
for edge cases: <https://www.lektoriranje.org/jezicni-savjetnik>.

## 1. Persiranje

Extends the register rule in `duh-hrvatskog.md` → Oslovljavanje. When you address the
reader with **Vi**, the agreement details are where copy quietly breaks:

- **Verb *and* participle go to plural** — and the participle takes the neutral
  (masculine) plural regardless of the reader's gender.
  - ❌ `Jeste li primila našu ponudu?` → ✅ `Jeste li primili našu ponudu?`
  - ❌ `Što ste rekla?` → ✅ `Što ste rekli?`
  Mixing plural verb with singular participle (`Jeste li dobila…`) is the most common
  persiranje error and instantly reads as unproofed.
- **Conditional: `Vi biste`, not `Vi bi`.** `Vi bi` is colloquial. Standard copy uses
  `biste` (2nd pl.) and `bismo` (1st pl.).
  - ❌ `Vi bi trebali…` → ✅ `Vi biste trebali…` · ✅ `Rado bismo Vam pomogli`
- **Sign-off: `lijep pozdrav`, not `lijepi pozdrav`.** It's the indefinite adjective
  (*kakav* pozdrav), so `lijep` — the standard close for a contact page or e-mail.

## 2. Kalkovi

Reinforces `duh-hrvatskog.md` → Administrativni stil with the exact verdicts:

- **`od strane` + genitiv → avoid.** It's an English-style passive calque. Make the
  agent the subject and go active.
  - ❌ `Projekt je izveden od strane naše tvrtke` → ✅ `Projekt je izvela naša tvrtka`
  - ❌ `Ugovor potpisan od strane investitora` → ✅ `Ugovor su potpisali investitori`
- **`vršiti` → `obavljati` / `raditi` / a precise verb.** `Vršiti` belongs to
  agriculture (vrši se žito); everything else is done, not "vršeno".
  - ❌ `vršimo popravke` → ✅ `popravljamo` · ❌ `vršimo otkup` → ✅ `otkupljujemo`
- **`obzirom da` / `s obzirom da` → `s obzirom na to da`** (the only complete standard
  form), or rephrase with `budući da` / `jer`.
  - ❌ `S obzirom da gradimo brzo…` → ✅ `Budući da gradimo brzo…`
- **`ukoliko` → `ako`.** `Ukoliko` is correct *only* paired with `utoliko`; everywhere
  else use `ako`. → ✅ `Ako trebate ponudu, javite se.`
- **`najoptimalnije` → `optimalno`.** Pleonasm: *optimalno* is already the best; it
  takes no comparative or superlative (no `optimalniji`, no `najoptimalnije`).

## 3. Izbor riječi

- **`zbog` (uzrok) vs `radi` (svrha).** `Zbog` = because of; `radi` = for the purpose
  of. The frequent error is `radi` for a cause.
  - ✅ `Zatvoreno zbog blagdana` (cause) · ✅ `Radi lakšeg snalaženja…` (purpose)
- **`tijekom`, not `tokom`.** For elapsed time use `tijekom` (`tijekom gradnje`); `tok`
  refers to a flow/current (rijeke).
- **`pogreška` vs `greška`.** `Pogreška` = a technical mistake / something done wrong —
  the right word for UI and form errors (`pogreška pri slanju`). `Greška` = a flaw or
  defect, weightier. So microcopy says `pogreška`, not `greška`.
- **`preporučujem`, not `preporučam`.** From `preporučivati` (ongoing). `Preporučam` is
  nonstandard. → ✅ `Preporučujemo…`
- **`elektronička pošta` / `e-pošta`, not `elektronska`.** `Elektronički` = relating to
  electronics (`e-pošta`, `e-knjiga`, `e-račun`); `elektronski` = relating to electrons.
- **`kontaktirati` — both `nas` and `s nama` are correct.** `Kontaktirajte nas`
  (accusative, = make contact) is the standard, idiomatic CTA. `kontaktirati s nekim`
  (instrumental) = to be in contact with. Either works; keep it consistent.
- **`radnik` and `djelatnik` are both correct.** `Radnik` is fully standard and natural
  — no need to reflexively euphemize to `djelatnik`. (`90 iskusnih djelatnika` is fine;
  so is `radnika`.)
- **`lozinka` / `zaporka` (password).** Both standard; `zaporka` is the puristic term
  common in official Croatian UIs, `lozinka` the everyday one. `šifra` leans "code".
- **`brend` is allowed.** A domesticated loanword — write `brend` in normal copy; keep
  `brand` (italicized) only when referring to the English term itself.

## 4. Pravopisne zamke

- **`sljedeći` (next) vs `slijedeći` (following).** `Sljedeći` is the adjective —
  *koji*: `sljedeći korak`, `sljedeća stranica`. `Slijedeći` is the gerund — *kako*
  (slijedeći upute). For a Next button / pagination it's **`sljedeći`**. Very common
  slip.
- **`neću` (written together).** Negations are normally separate (`ne znam`, `ne mogu`),
  but `neću` — like `nemam`, `nisam`, `nemoj` — is one word.
- **`bit će` / `radit će`, not `biti će` / `raditi će`.** When the unstressed `će`
  follows the infinitive, the infinitive drops its `-i`: `radit ćemo`, `bit će gotovo`.
- **`sa mnom`, not `samnom`** — always two words: `s tobom`, `s njim`, `s nama`,
  `s vama`, `s njima`.
- **`s` vs `sa`.** Use `sa` only before words starting `s, š, z, ž` (or an awkward
  cluster): `sa mnom`, `sa zadovoljstvom`, `sa pšenicom`. Otherwise `s`: `s vama`,
  `s timom`.
- **`milijun`, not `milion` / `miljon`** — the standard form (`1,5 milijuna €`).
- **`podaci` and `podatci` are both correct** (`podatci` slightly preferred in writing,
  `podaci` more common and fully acceptable). `Podaci o tvrtki` is fine.

## 5. Interpunkcija

- **Bullet / vertical lists (uspravno nabrajanje):** each item starts **lowercase**
  (unless a proper noun), there is **no punctuation at the end of items**, and a single
  **period closes the last item only**. No commas or semicolons after items.
  ```
  Nudimo:
  - građevinske radove
  - proizvodnju betona
  - instalaterske i strojarske radove.
  ```
  (This is the most-violated rule in Croatian feature lists — most people wrongly add a
  comma or semicolon after each line.)
- **Dates:** `24. svibnja 2026.` (month genitive, lowercase) · numeric `24. 5. 2026.`
  (spaces after the periods) · roman `24. V. 2026.` (period after the numeral).
  Wrong: `24.5.2026.`, `24. svibanj 2026.`, `June 24, 2026`.
- **Slash `/`:** no spaces for either/or single words and ranges (`s/sa`, `i/ili`,
  `student/studentica`, `2024./2025.`); **with** spaces when a side is multi-word
  (`predavač / viši predavač`).
- **`internet`** — lowercase; a common noun like `televizija`, `radio`.
- **`e-` compounds** — hyphen, lowercase: `e-pošta`, `e-knjiga`, `e-račun`.

## 6. Slaganje

- **Match the relative pronoun to the nearest noun.** After two nouns joined by `i`,
  `koji/koja/koje` agrees with the noun right before it. If it matches only the first
  noun, the reader takes the clause as describing just that noun, and the second one
  drops out of the sentence.
  - ❌ `Gradimo web stranice i sadržaj koje tražilice razumiju` → ✅ `Gradimo web stranice i sadržaj koji tražilice razumiju`
  - ❌ `Dizajniramo ambalažu i logotip koju kupci pamte` → ✅ `Dizajniramo ambalažu i logotip koji kupci pamte`
  - ❌ `Opišite problem i poruku o grešci koji se pojavljuje` → ✅ `Opišite problem i poruku o grešci koja se pojavljuje`

  Exception: when the clause clearly describes both nouns together as its subject, a
  masculine plural is also correct for mixed genders (`servis i dijagnostika koji traju
  jedan dan`). And `koje` is also the masculine plural accusative
  (`odgovore i podatke koje stranica nudi`) — don't "fix" that one.
