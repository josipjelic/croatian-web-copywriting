# Naslovi i pozivi na akciju — headlines & CTAs

The two highest-leverage elements on any page: the headline that decides whether the
reader stays, and the CTA that decides whether they act. Read this before writing or
fixing either. All examples follow the rules in `duh-hrvatskog.md` (sentence case,
chosen register, no translationese).

## 1. The stack

Croatian section headers usually come as a three-part stack. Each part has a job;
don't let them repeat each other.

- **Eyebrow (nadnaslov)** — 1–3 words, orients the reader, sets category. Sentence
  case or small-caps. `Naše usluge` · `O tvrtki` · `Vlastita betonara` · `Brojke koje
  govore`. Keep it a label, not a sentence.
- **Headline (naslov, H1/H2)** — the promise or claim. One line, benefit-first.
  `Nudimo cjelokupnu uslugu gradnje` · `Iskustvo izgrađeno na rezultatima`.
- **Lead (podnaslov / uvod)** — 1–2 sentences that pay off the headline with substance
  and usually carry the first CTA. `Od arhitektonskog nacrta do potpune realizacije —
  vodimo Vaš projekt kroz svaku fazu.`

The mistake to avoid: eyebrow and headline saying the same thing
(`Usluge` / `Naše usluge`). The eyebrow categorizes; the headline promises.

## 2. Naslovi

A headline earns the next five seconds of attention. In Croatian, four things make it
work:

**1. Lead with the reader's benefit, not the company's identity.**
- ✅ `Gradimo pouzdano — od temelja do krova` (what you get)
- ❌ `L Projekt d.o.o. — građevinska tvrtka` (who we are — nobody cares yet)

**2. Be specific. Specifics are the native Croatian flex** (see `duh-hrvatskog.md` →
Ton). A number or concrete noun beats any adjective.
- ✅ `Iskustvo izgrađeno na rezultatima` + `200+ izgrađenih objekata`
- ❌ `Bogato iskustvo i visoka kvaliteta`

**3. Clarity over cleverness.** A headline the reader has to decode has already lost.
Wordplay is a bonus *after* the meaning lands, never instead of it.

**4. Sentence case, one idea, no Title Case, no hype stack.** If you've used two
adjectives, cut one. (`vrhunska, inovativna i pouzdana rješenja` → name the thing.)

The em-dash split is a workhorse for Croatian headlines: a claim, a dash, the proof or
texture. `Beton po mjeri — brzo, kvalitetno i povoljno` · `Pouzdan partner u
graditeljstvu od 2017.`

## 3. Formulas

Starting points, not straitjackets. Each produces sentence-case, native Croatian.

| Formula | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Glagol + korist** (verb + benefit) | Lead with an action verb | `Gradimo pouzdano — od temelja do krova` |
| **Korist + dokaz** (claim — proof) | Promise, em dash, evidence | `Iskustvo izgrađeno na rezultatima` |
| **Za koga + što** (for whom + what) | Name the audience | `Cjelokupna usluga gradnje` |
| **Što + kako** (what + how, "po mjeri") | The thing + its quality | `Beton po mjeri — brzo, kvalitetno i povoljno` |
| **Partner / od kada** (trust + tenure) | Relationship + longevity | `Pouzdan partner u graditeljstvu od 2017.` |
| **Ponos / rezultat** (pride in results) | For portfolio/projects | `Realizacije na koje smo ponosni` |

For a hero H1 specifically, aim to convey **what you do + the payoff** in one line, with
the lead carrying *for whom* and *the proof*. Example pairing:
- H1: `Gradimo pouzdano — od temelja do krova`
- Lead: `L Projekt nudi cjelokupnu uslugu gradnje, vlastitu proizvodnju betona te
  instalaterske i strojarske radove — sve na jednom mjestu.`

## 4. Pozivi na akciju

A CTA is a small promise about what happens when the reader acts. In Croatian:

**1. It's an imperative in the site's register** (see `duh-hrvatskog.md` →
Oslovljavanje). Vi: `Zatražite ponudu`. ti: `Zatraži ponudu`. Be consistent across
every button on the site.

**2. Name the action or the value, never the mechanic.** `Klikni ovdje`/`Pošalji` (bare)
tell the reader nothing. `Zatražite ponudu`, `Preuzmite katalog`, `Pogledajte projekte`
tell them exactly what they get.

**3. Reduce the risk.** A short reassurance next to the button lifts conversion and
reads as confident: `Besplatno i bez obveze` · `Odgovaramo u roku od 24 sata` ·
`Bez skrivenih troškova`.

**4. One primary CTA per view.** Give it the strongest verb; make secondary actions
visibly lighter. A common, clean pairing:
- Primary: `Zatražite ponudu`
- Secondary: `Pogledajte projekte`

**5. Match the CTA to the page's moment.** A cold homepage hero earns a low-commitment
ask (`Saznajte više`, `Pogledajte projekte`); a contact page can ask directly
(`Pošaljite upit`). Don't ask for marriage on the first screen.

**First-person variant:** Croatian can use a first-person button (`Želim ponudu`,
`Želim besplatnu procjenu`) for a warmer, reader-voiced feel. It works, but use it
sparingly and never mix it with second-person buttons on the same site.

## 5. CTA verb bank

Native Croatian CTA verbs, in the **Vi** form (drop the `-te` for **ti**:
`Zatražite`→`Zatraži`). Pick the one that names the actual next step.

| Intent | CTA (Vi) |
|---|---|
| Get a quote | `Zatražite ponudu` · `Zatražite besplatnu procjenu` |
| Send an inquiry | `Pošaljite upit` · `Javite nam se` |
| Contact | `Kontaktirajte nas` · `Stupite u kontakt` |
| Learn more | `Saznajte više` |
| See work / portfolio | `Pogledajte projekte` · `Pogledajte realizacije` |
| Download | `Preuzmite katalog` · `Preuzmite cjenik` |
| Get started / begin | `Krenite` · `Započnite` · `Otvorite račun` |
| Book / schedule | `Rezervirajte termin` · `Dogovorite sastanak` |
| Subscribe | `Pretplatite se` · `Prijavite se na newsletter` |
| Buy / order | `Naručite` · `Kupite` |
| Call | `Nazovite nas` · `Nazovite: 091 622 3990` |

Avoid: `Klikni(te) ovdje`, `Submit`/`Pošalji` (bare), `Više` alone (too vague — say
`Saznajte više` or `Više o nama`).

## 6. Before / after

Real fixes, each showing the failure mode and the native rewrite.

**Hero headline — translationese → native**
- ❌ `Dobrodošli! Mi Smo Vaš Pouzdani Partner za Sve Vaše Građevinske Potrebe`
  (Title Case · "dobrodošli" filler · possessive plague · empty "sve vaše … potrebe")
- ✅ `Gradimo pouzdano — od temelja do krova`

**Section headline — vague → specific**
- ❌ `Bogato iskustvo i tradicija`
- ✅ `Iskustvo izgrađeno na rezultatima` (paired with `200+ izgrađenih objekata`)

**CTA — mechanic → value, with risk-reducer**
- ❌ `Pošalji` (bare button, informal, says nothing)
- ✅ `Pošaljite upit` + sublabel `Odgovaramo u roku od 24 sata`

**CTA — calque → native**
- ❌ `Započnite Sada` / `Saznajte Više` (Title Case calques of "Get Started" / "Learn More")
- ✅ `Zatražite ponudu` · `Saznajte više` (sentence case, concrete)

**Eyebrow + headline — repetition → division of labour**
- ❌ Eyebrow `Usluge` + H2 `Naše usluge` (same word twice)
- ✅ Eyebrow `Naše usluge` + H2 `Nudimo cjelokupnu uslugu gradnje`

**Betonara CTA — hype → concrete promise**
- ❌ `Najbolji beton po nevjerojatnim cijenama!`
- ✅ `Beton po mjeri — brzo, kvalitetno i povoljno` + `Kontaktirajte nas za ponudu.`
